### 2. DISCLOSURE AND INTER-CENTRE TRANSFER OF CLINICAL INFORMATION

* **Multi-Centre Collaboration**: To facilitate seamless continuity of care, I consent to the secure sharing of my diagnostic scans, treatment notes, and laboratory prescriptions among specialist clinicians within Jerush Groups and Hospitals across its regional centres (Thuckalay, Chennai, Trichy, and Dubai).
* **Authorized Third-Party Medical Partners**: I understand that Jerush Groups and Hospitals may disclose necessary data to vetted external dental laboratories (for custom CAD/CAM prosthetics, zirconia crowns, and surgical guides), pathology laboratories (for tissue biopsies), accredited cloud EHR infrastructure providers, and health insurance providers for claim settlement. All such third parties are contractually bound by strict data confidentiality covenants.
* **Legal and Regulatory Mandates**: Information may be disclosed to government health agencies or statutory authorities when mandatory under applicable Indian or UAE legislation.
* **Corporate Restructuring**: In the event of an institutional merger, regional branch consolidation, or organizational expansion, patient records may be transferred to the successor healthcare entity with identical confidentiality obligations.

### 3. RETENTION AND SECURE ARCHIVING OF CLINICAL RECORDS

* Clinical records, treatment charts, consent forms, and radiographic archives are retained in accordance with statutory hospital record-keeping regulations (minimum 7 years for adult clinical records or indefinitely in secure de-identified electronic archives).
* Jerush Groups and Hospitals enforces robust technical safeguards, including 256-bit database encryption, multi-factor authentication for clinical portals, and role-based staff access restrictions to prevent unauthorized disclosure.

### 4. FINANCIAL POLICIES, TREATMENT ESTIMATES & PAYMENTS

* **Upfront Transparent Estimates**: Jerush Groups and Hospitals maintains an uncompromising commitment to transparent pricing. Prior to the commencement of non-emergency elective procedures, patients receive an itemized treatment estimate detailing procedural fees, implant hardware costs, lab fabrication charges, and post-operative follow-up visits.
* **Payment Schedules**: Major rehabilitations (such as full-mouth dental implants or orthognathic surgery) adhere to milestone-based payment schedules agreed upon prior to treatment initiation.
* **Cancellation & Rescheduling**: Notice of appointment cancellation or rescheduling must be communicated at least 24 hours in advance to optimize surgical theatre and specialist consultation scheduling.

### 5. PATIENT RIGHTS AND CONSENT WITHDRAWAL

* **Right of Access & Portability**: Patients have the legal right to inspect and obtain certified copies of their clinical notes, digital CBCT scans, and diagnostic radiographs upon written request.
* **Voluntary Information**: Patients are entitled to withhold non-essential personal information; however, withholding critical medical history (such as cardiovascular conditions, bleeding disorders, or medication history) may compel Jerush clinicians to decline treatment for patient safety reasons.
* **Consent Withdrawal**: Patients may withdraw consent for marketing notices, educational newsletters, or secondary health communications at any time by contacting our patient care desk. Statutory clinical retention requirements remain unaffected.

### 6. INVESTIGATION SAMPLES, DE-IDENTIFIED DATA & ACADEMIC RESEARCH

* Diagnostic tissue specimens (e.g. biopsies) are processed strictly for pathological diagnosis. Any residual sample or anonymized radiographic series may be utilized by Jerush clinical faculty for medical education, surgical case conferences, or academic publications strictly in a de-identified format where patient identity cannot be ascertained.
* Patients may explicitly opt out of academic/research use of de-identified data at any time without compromising their access to clinical care.
