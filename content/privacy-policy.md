---

### 1. OUR COMMITMENT TO PATIENT & VISITOR PRIVACY

**Jerush Groups and Hospitals** (“Jerush”, “We”, “Us”, or “Our”) is committed to protecting the privacy, dignity, and confidentiality of every individual who interacts with us—whether as a patient in our clinical suites or as a visitor exploring our digital platforms.

We adhere strictly to applicable health data protection laws, including the **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India)**, statutory healthcare regulations of Tamil Nadu, and applicable healthcare data confidentiality frameworks in the **United Arab Emirates (UAE)** across our Fujairah and Dubai operations.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, store, process, secure, and handle your Personal and Sensitive Health Information when you visit our website, use our appointment booking portals, or receive medical, dental, or aesthetic care at our centres.

---

### 2. WHAT INFORMATION WE COLLECT

We collect only data necessary to deliver exceptional clinical care, ensure patient safety, and maintain reliable digital services:

* **Identity & Demographic Details**: Full name, age, date of birth, gender, marital status, and government-issued identification (where required for surgical records or insurance);
* **Contact Coordinates**: Mobile phone number, WhatsApp contact, email address, residential address, postal code, and emergency contact / caregiver details;
* **Clinical & Diagnostic Records**: Detailed medical and dental histories, known drug allergies, surgical records, systemic health parameters (e.g. blood pressure, diabetic indices), prescriptions, intra-oral photographs, 3D CBCT radiographs, and pathological lab findings;
* **Digital Interaction Data**: Consultation request notes, preferred clinic branch (Thuckalay, Chennai, Trichy, Dubai), preferred specialist doctor, and feedback surveys;
* **Financial & Transactional Records**: Payment receipts, insurance pre-authorization documents, payment mode tokens, and billing histories (note: sensitive credit/debit card numbers are processed directly by RBI/UAE-licensed payment gateways and are never stored on our servers);
* **Technical & Browser Telemetry**: IP address, device type, browser language, referring URLs, operating system, and anonymous analytical cookies to ensure responsive website performance.

---

### 3. HOW WE COLLECT YOUR DATA

1. **Direct In-Clinic Intake**: Completed patient registration cards, physical consultation notes, diagnostic consent forms, and medical questionnaires;
2. **Digital Appointment Portals**: Forms submitted through www.jerushdentoface.com, WhatsApp official business channels, or telephonic booking desks;
3. **Diagnostic Suites**: High-precision 3D digital imaging, intra-oral scanning (e.g. for Jerushaligne clear aligners), and clinical photography before and after aesthetic procedures;
4. **Automated Technologies**: Standard functional cookies and telemetry used strictly to optimize website navigation, security, and load speeds.

---

### 4. PURPOSE AND LEGAL BASIS FOR PROCESSING

Your information is processed strictly for legitimate clinical, administrative, and legal purposes:
* **Healthcare Delivery**: Conducting clinical examinations, planning dental implant surgery, designing orthodontic appliances, administering laser skin therapy, and dispensing medications;
* **Unified Electronic Health Records (EHR)**: Enabling our multi-specialty clinicians to safely review diagnostic history during cross-branch visits (e.g. consultations between Thuckalay and Chennai);
* **Communication & Care Alerts**: Transmitting appointment confirmations, treatment countdowns, post-operative oral care instructions, and emergency alerts via phone, SMS, WhatsApp, or email;
* **Patient Safety & Pharmacovigilance**: Monitoring for potential adverse drug interactions, anaesthetic contraindications, or post-operative complications;
* **Statutory Compliance**: Fulfilling mandatory reporting obligations under the Clinical Establishments Act, Dental Council regulations, and public health authorities.

---

### 5. DATA SHARING AND TRANSFER SAFEGUARDS

* **No Commercial Sale of Data**: Jerush Groups and Hospitals **never** sells, rents, or monetizes patient personal or medical data to advertisers, pharmaceutical marketers, or data brokers.
* **Authorized Medical Collaborators**: Clinical data may be shared under strict confidentiality agreements with certified dental laboratories (for custom crown/bridge milling), accredited diagnostic pathology labs, and authorized cloud EHR infrastructure providers.
* **Legal Compulsion**: Data may be disclosed to law enforcement or judicial bodies only when officially compelled by a valid legal subpoena or court order under applicable law.
* **Cross-Border Continuity**: For international patients receiving consultations in Dubai and continuing care in India (or vice-versa), records are transferred securely using encrypted protocols with verified patient authorization.

---

### 6. DATA SECURITY AND TECHNICAL MEASURES

We employ rigorous, hospital-grade cybersecurity standards to safeguard your sensitive records:
* End-to-end Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3) encryption across all web forms and digital transactions;
* Role-based access controls ensuring only authorized treating doctors and clinical nurses can view confidential patient health charts;
* Regular security audits, vulnerability scanning, and multi-factor authentication on hospital servers;
* Air-gapped, encrypted daily backups of all diagnostic radiographic repositories.

---

### 7. COOKIE POLICY & ANALYTICS

Our website uses small text files called cookies to:
* Ensure secure website navigation and form functioning;
* Remember your clinic location preference for faster page loading;
* Analyze aggregated, non-personally identifiable traffic patterns to improve site accessibility.

You have complete control over cookies through your web browser settings. Disabling functional cookies may limit certain interactive booking tools on our website.

---

### 8. RETENTION OF MEDICAL & PERSONAL RECORDS

* In compliance with statutory medical council guidelines, clinical records and diagnostic images are maintained for a minimum statutory period (typically 7 years, or longer for paediatric and surgical patients).
* Non-clinical web inquiry inquiries are retained only for as long as necessary to address your consultation request or grievance, after which they are securely purged.

---

### 9. YOUR LEGAL RIGHTS AS A PATIENT

Subject to statutory exemptions under medical record retention laws, you are entitled to:
1. **Right to Access**: Request a certified digital copy of your medical charts, treatment notes, and CBCT scans;
2. **Right to Rectification**: Correct any inaccurate demographic, emergency contact, or insurance details;
3. **Right to Withdraw Consent**: Revoke consent for non-essential communications (such as clinic health newsletters or wellness alerts) at any time;
4. **Right to Privacy of Case Media**: Ensure that any clinical before-and-after photographs used for educational purposes are published only with explicit written consent and de-identification where requested.
