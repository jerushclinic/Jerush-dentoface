## 1. How Built-In Sliding Doors Change Your Treatment

Self-ligating braces replace old-fashioned elastic rubber bands with micro-engineered sliding doors built directly into each bracket. 

Instead of tying the wire tightly against your teeth, these sliding doors let the wire slide smoothly back and forth. This low-friction design allows your teeth to move into place naturally with far less pressure, making your treatment much more comfortable.

### Key Patient Benefits
* **Fewer Clinic Visits:** Because the sliding doors do not stretch out or wear down like rubber bands, your wire keeps working effectively for up to 8 weeks.
* **Easier Brushing & Flossing:** Without rubber bands trapping food, keeping your teeth clean after meals takes half the effort.
* **Shorter Appointment Times:** Swapping wires takes just a few minutes, our orthodontists simply click the door open, change the wire and snap it closed.

## 4. Your Treatment Journey in 3 Clear Phases

Rather than a basic checklist, your progress with self-ligating braces follows three planned milestones:

### Phase 1: Uncrowding & Arch Unlocking (Months 1–4)
Ultra-flexible shape-memory wires apply gentle, continuous pressure to untangle crowded teeth and open up tight spaces without heavy force.

### Phase 2: Active Alignment & Gap Closure (Months 5–9)
Stronger structural wires level your dental arch, close remaining spaces and align how your upper and lower teeth meet when you chew.

### Phase 3: Fine-Tuning & Permanent Retention (Months 10–12+)
Our orthodontists make precise micro-adjustments to perfect your smile line before removing the brackets and fitting custom retainers to lock in your results.

## 5. Clear Answers to Common Patient Questions

### Q1: Do self-ligating braces hurt less than traditional braces?
Yes. Because self-ligating brackets use a sliding clip instead of tight elastic ties, the wire applies a lighter, continuous force. Most patients experience significantly less soreness after monthly visits compared to standard braces.

### Q2: How often will I need to visit the clinic for checkups?
You will typically need adjustment appointments only once every **6 to 8 weeks**. This makes self-ligating braces ideal for busy professionals, college students, or patients traveling from other cities across Tamil Nadu.

### Q3: Are self-ligating braces available in clear ceramic?
Yes! You can choose clear self-ligating ceramic brackets that match your natural enamel while keeping all the speed and convenience of the built-in sliding mechanism.

### Q4: Can self-ligating braces prevent the need for tooth extractions?
In many cases, yes. Because self-ligating braces produce very low friction, they can widen narrow arches and create space naturally better than standard braces. While severe crowding may still require extractions, many patients can achieve a straight smile without pulling healthy teeth.

### Q5: How do self-ligating braces compare in cost to traditional braces?
Self-ligating braces have a slightly higher upfront cost due to the high-precision engineered brackets. However, because you require fewer total visits and complete your treatment faster, many patients find it much more convenient. Jerush Dentofacial offers flexible **0% interest monthly payment options (EMI)** across all our centers to help fit your budget.

### Q6: How do I maintain good oral hygiene with these braces daily?
Cleaning self-ligating braces is simple and straightforward:
* **Brush after meals:** Use a soft-bristled orthodontic toothbrush to clear away food particles.
* **Use an interdental micro-brush:** Clean underneath the main wire once a day to keep gums healthy.
* **Rinse with fluoride mouthwash:** Helps protect your enamel and keeps your breath fresh throughout treatment.
