## 1. How Lingual Braces Stay Completely Hidden

Standard braces are glued to the front of your teeth. Lingual braces are glued to the back (tongue side) of your teeth. 

Because teeth are smooth on the front but bumpy and irregular on the back, off-the-shelf brackets will not fit. We scan your mouth in 3D and send those files to a lab where every individual bracket is cast to match the exact shape of your inner teeth. Once glued into place, the wire pulls your teeth into line from behind.

## 2. Who Chooses Hidden Braces?

Most adults and teens who pick lingual braces want straight teeth without the visual disruption of front brackets or the daily hassle of removable plastic trays.

* **People in Public Roles:** Actors, TV hosts, news anchors, models and flight attendants who cannot have visible metal on camera.
* **Working Professionals:** Corporate managers, lawyers, sales professionals and teachers who give frequent presentations.
* **Brides & Grooms:** People preparing for upcoming weddings, engagement shoots or family celebrations.
* **Forgetful Aligner Wearers:** Anyone who knows they will lose clear plastic aligner trays or forget to put them back in after eating.

## 6. Alignment Issues Lingual Braces Can Fix

Lingual braces exert the exact same pull as traditional braces. They fix almost all common tooth alignment problems, including:

* **Crowded & Overlapping Teeth:** Separates tightly packed teeth and pulls them into a clean arch.
* **Teeth Gaps:** Closes gaps between front or side teeth cleanly.
* **Deep Overbites:** Fixes upper front teeth that cover too much of your lower teeth when biting down.
* **Crossbites:** Corrects teeth that bite on the inside of your lower jaw line.
* **Twisted Canines:** Rotates turned teeth into place without needing front extractions in mild cases.

## 7. What to Expect (Treatment Timeline)

### Stage 1: 3D Scan & Custom Lab Build (Weeks 1 to 3)
We take a 3D digital scan of your mouth. We send the digital model to the lab where your custom brackets are cast and custom archwires are bent by robotic equipment.

### Stage 2: Fitting & Speech Practice (Weeks 4 to 6)
We bond the custom brackets to the back of your teeth and fit your first flexible wire. We give you soft dental wax to cover any brackets that rub your tongue during the first week.

### Stage 3: Monthly Adjustments & Final Retainers (Months 2 to 14+)
You visit us every 6 to 8 weeks to check your progress and get fresh wires. When treatment is done, we remove the inner brackets and fit custom clear retainers to hold your teeth in position.

## 8. Daily Cleaning & Care Tips

Cleaning braces attached to the back of your teeth requires a few extra steps:

* **Brush from Below:** Angle your toothbrush upward toward your tongue so the bristles get under the brackets.
* **Use a Water Flosser:** A water flosser pushes a jet of water behind your teeth, flushing out trapped food particles in seconds.
* **Use Micro Interdental Brushes:** Pass a thin wire micro-brush under the main wire once a day to clear away plaque.

## 9. Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Can anyone see lingual braces when I talk or laugh?
No. The brackets sit on the back side of your teeth facing your tongue. Unless someone stands behind you while you tilt your head back with your mouth wide open, they will not see anything.

### Q2: Will lingual braces affect my speech or give me a lisp?
Yes, most patients have a minor lisp for the first 1 to 2 weeks because the tongue hits the back brackets when pronouncing certain words (like "S" or "T"). Reading aloud at home for 10 minutes a day helps your tongue adapt quickly.

### Q3: Why do lingual braces cost more than front metal braces?
Every lingual bracket must be individually custom-made in a dental lab to match the exact shape of your inner teeth. Placing them also requires specialized orthodontic training and custom wire bending. We offer **0% interest monthly EMI options** to help keep payments simple.

### Q4: Will lingual braces hurt my tongue?
Your tongue will feel tender for the first few days because it touches the brackets constantly when swallowing. Putting a tiny piece of soft dental wax over any rough spots keeps your tongue comfortable while it gets used to the new space.

### Q5: How often do I need to visit the clinic for checkups?
Most patients come in for wire changes once every **6 to 8 weeks**, making it convenient if you live in another town or travel regularly across Tamil Nadu.

### Q6: Will removing lingual braces damage my inner teeth?
No. When your treatment is finished, we pop the brackets off cleanly using a specialized removal tool. We then polish off the remaining glue, leaving your inner tooth enamel smooth and clean.
