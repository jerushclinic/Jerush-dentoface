### Icon Production Specifications

When generating or commissioning the 3D icons, ensure they follow these visual standards to match existing hospital assets:
- **Format**: `.webp` (lossless or 95% quality, transparent alpha channel background)
- **Resolution**: `256 x 256 px` (standard display renders at `32 x 32 px` to `48 x 48 px`)
- **Aesthetic Style**: Premium 3D isometric or front-angled medical illustration with soft studio lighting, clinical cyan/blue/white accents, and clean shadow depth (similar to `clear-aligner.webp`, `dental-implant.webp`, `hydrafacial.webp`).
- **File Naming**: Lowercase alphanumeric with hyphens (e.g., `pulpectomy.webp`).
